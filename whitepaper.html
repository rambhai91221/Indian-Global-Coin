1Executive Summary

Indian Global Coin (IGC) is a community-driven BEP-20 token deployed on the BNB Smart Chain (BSC). It is designed to serve as a fast, secure and ultra-low-cost medium of exchange for everyday digital payments, remittances and cross-border commerce.

IGC is live and trading today on PancakeSwap — the largest decentralized exchange on BNB Smart Chain — with public liquidity, live charts on DexScreener and PooCoin, and a fully verified smart contract on BscScan.

By combining the speed and affordability of BSC with a simple, transparent token model, IGC aims to bridge Indian value systems with the global crypto economy — enabling anyone, anywhere to send and receive value in seconds, for less than the cost of a cup of chai.

2Vision

To build a borderless digital payment ecosystem where IGC becomes a trusted medium of exchange for daily life — from a local shop purchase to an international remittance — without banks, without borders, and without delays.

  • Make crypto payments as simple as sending a message.
  • Empower small merchants, freelancers and families with near-zero fees.
  • Represent Indian innovation on the global blockchain stage.

3The Problem

  • Slow transfers: International bank transfers take 2–5 working days.
  • High fees: Remittance and cross-border payments can cost 5–10% in fees and hidden FX spreads.
  • Financial exclusion: Millions remain outside the traditional banking net.
  • Limited utility: Most cryptocurrencies today are traded, not used.

4Our Solution

  • Instant settlement: Transactions confirm on BSC in 3–5 seconds.
  • Micro-fees: Gas cost is usually less than ₹1 per transaction.
  • On-chain transparency: Every transfer is publicly verifiable on BscScan.
  • Merchant-friendly: QR-based payments for shops, cafés and online stores.
  • Easy onboarding: Anyone with a Trust Wallet or MetaMask can use IGC today.
